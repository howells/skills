# Font recommendations

Curated faces for interface work (body, labels, data, mono) and brand work (headlines, identity, editorial). `chiaroscuro`, `maquette` and `paste-up` each bundle a condensed copy of this file as their own font guidance; change the selection policy or the recommended faces there in the same edit.

## Selection

Keep the product's existing faces unless the task requires a change. Choose a new face from the recommendations below, suited to its job and the real content. One family in several weights is often enough; add a second only for genuine contrast. Check the actual font files, licence, available weights and the rendered result.

## Never use

These signal that nobody made a choice. Never pick them for a new selection, in UI or brand work; only an explicit user instruction overrides this.

- **Instrument Serif**: the number one AI-slop serif, on every generated landing page.
- **Playfair Display**: template-tier.
- **Poppins**: indistinguishable from a thousand other sites.
- **Montserrat**: overexposed.
- **Roboto**: the Android default; choosing it says "I didn't choose."
- **Arial**: the non-choice.

## Finding pairings

Before committing to a face or pairing, look it up on Fonts In Use (fontsinuse.com) and Typewolf (typewolf.com, especially its "similar fonts" and "pairings") to see it in shipped work. A pairing that works in a specimen can fail in an interface.

## Pairing

- One face talks (display or brand), the other stays quiet.
- Contrast on at least one axis: serif and sans, geometric and humanist, condensed display and wide body. Never pair two similar faces, such as two geometric sans.
- Weight and width make hierarchy more than family does.
- Judge at real sizes; a face that sings at 72px can fail at 16px.
